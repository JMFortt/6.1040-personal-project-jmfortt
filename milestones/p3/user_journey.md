# TAble User Journey

## Writeup

Let's say I'm a faculty member in charge of a class. I can register an account and create a class with whatever name I choose. Once other users have registered, I can invite them to join my class as TAs, LAs or staff using their usernames. I can see the status of open invites, and withdraw them if I want. Once they accept, the TAs and staff can set up a master schedule, assign people to its shifts, and publish weekly copies. LAs have more limited abilities, but also can use the tool to make their lives easier. For example, LA 1 can request a cover for their shift, LA 2 can claim it, and a TA or staff member can approve it. That week's schedule then shows LA 2 scheduled for the covered shift, and the request would be marked as approved. During the shift, a TA or staff member can mark LAs present, and LAs can see their current attendance record for the class. Cover requests, statuses, etc. are shown on a convenient dashboard. Attendance can be easily marked from a separate attendance dashboard, or on each schedule copy (both update live).
