# TAble User Journey

## Writeup

Let's say I'm a faculty member in charge of a class. I can register an account, create a class called "6.123," and create a TA role within with permissions (edit schedule, approve requests, mark attendance), as well as an LA role with different permissions (view schedule, request cover, view attendance). I invite a TA and two LAs to join the class, who register and accept. The TA creates a few shifts and assigns the LAs to them. LA 1 requests a cover for their shift, LA 2 claims it, and the TA approves it. The schedule would then show LA 2 scheduled for the covered shift, and the request would be marked as approved. During the shift, the TA can mark LA 2 present, and LA 2 can see their current attendance record.
