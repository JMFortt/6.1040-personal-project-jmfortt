# Section 5: User Journey

Let's say I'm a faculty member in a class with 5 TAs and 20 LAs. The semester is starting soon and I want to set up the office hour schedule. I could use Google Sheets, Slack, a Claude-bot or some amalgamation of the three, but instead I can use TAble.

I can go to TAble and set up an account. Once I do and log in, I'm prompted to either "create a class" or "join a class." I click create a class, give it a name, and create roles for TAs and LAs, with different permissions for each. I give LAs permission to see the schedule, request swaps and request covers. I give TAs the same permissions, as well as the permission to make schedule edits and mark attendance. I invite the newly-hired TAs and LAs to join my new class on TAble, and once they do I assign shifts on the site - giving everyone one place to reference the schedule.

Any swaps or covers that come up during the semester are handled exclusively on the site, with no need for external communication or documentation. During shifts, attendance is marked on the site live, with everyone able to easily verify not only who was/is/has been present, but also, for those actively on shift, that they themselves have been marked present.
